## Original-resolution human review

### Desktop shared shell

The four-domain rail, AI local navigation, account-truth band, queue / mission / context topology, bottom command surface, and bounded AI 客服 all occupy the approved reading order and broadly matching proportions at both widths. Real production-shaped labels and controls create visible local typography and horizontal-position differences. These are recorded differences; the diagnostic comparator does not erase them. No Desktop shell difference observed in this foundation review independently blocks the next plan.

### APP shared shell — material blocker

The production APP shell is not yet spatially faithful to the approved Mobile AI Mission concept:

- the production identity and Account Truth area occupies two taller layers instead of the concept’s concise identity plus compact truth composition;
- production inserts a full-width `Context / Proof` control row that is not present at that position in the approved reading order;
- those two changes push the Mission workspace materially downward at both `390x844` and `430x932`;
- within the same first viewport, the approved concept exposes `需要你`, `账户影响`, and `最近完成`, while production reaches only the enlarged Mission / 决策摘要 content;
- the resulting density, first-screen hierarchy, and relationship between truth, mission, attention, and account impact are materially different;
- the four-domain bottom navigation and floating read-only AI 客服 use the approved language, but they do not offset the upper-shell mismatch.

Verdict: **material Foundation APP shell difference; Plan 02 remains blocked.** Task 7 does not modify production UI to conceal or repair this finding.
