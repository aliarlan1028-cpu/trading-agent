THE DOCTRINE

Five lenses, weighed on every trade

Alignment across the five decides conviction — and conviction decides size. Weak alignment isn't a reason to skip; it's a reason to trade smaller.

Signal alignment → position size
📐
Structure — SMC (4H/1H)

BOS / CHoCH, supply-demand zones, 1H liquidity sweeps. The structure-invalidation level becomes the stop.

🔬
Microstructure

Funding vs the coin's own P85, order-book imbalance, spread (bps), √-participation impact cost, OI change.

🐋
Smart money

Top-trader long/short ratio, retail crowding as a contra-signal, taker buy/sell flow.

🌐
Events & news

Web-searches real catalysts in the trade window — forced to say “none” rather than invent one.

📊
Multi-timeframe

High timeframe sets direction (position + slope + swing), low timeframe finds the entry level.
